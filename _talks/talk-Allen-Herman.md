name: My research in group rings and association schemes&#58; past, present (and future?)
speakers:
 - Allen Herman

 - Plenary
---

I will begin with a historical walk through the prominent themes of my past research: Schur indices for finite groups, equivariant Brauer groups, units of integral group rings, and representation theory of association schemes.   I will then discuss the open problems motivating my present research: the cyclotomic eigenvalue problem for schemes, representation theory of Terwilliger algebras, and (still) some problems involving units of group rings and Schur indices.  I will close with what I hope to be some nuggets of wisdom as we collectively try to navigate an uncertain future of mathematics research.
