We study the association schemes arising from strongly-regular graphs whose Terwilliger algebras are as small as possible. A strongly-regular graph $$\Gamma$$ is called triply transitive if its automorphism group $$\operatorname{Aut}(\Gamma)$$ is transitive, and its Terwilliger algebra coincides with both its subspace $$T_0$$ and the centralizer algebra of a point stabilizer of $$\operatorname{Aut}(\Gamma)$$. In this talk, I will present a complete classification of triply-transitive strongly-regular graphs.

This is joint work with Allen Herman and Sarobidy Razafimahatratra.
