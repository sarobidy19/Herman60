---
name: Raising and lowering maps for tridiagonal pairs
speakers:
 - Paul Terwilliger  
categories:
 - Invited
---

A tridiagonal pair is an ordered pair of diagonalizable linear maps $$A$$, $$B$$
on a nonzero finite-dimensional vector space $$V$$, that each act in a block-tridiagonal fashion
on the eigenspaces of the other one. We consider the $$A$$-eigenspace decomposition of $$V$$, the
$$B$$-eigenspace decomposition of $$V$$, and a third decomposition of $$V$$, said to be split.
Each of these decompositions comes with its own raising map and lowering map. We will explain how
all these raising and lowering maps are related to each other. We will also give some injectivity/surjectivity
results about these maps.
