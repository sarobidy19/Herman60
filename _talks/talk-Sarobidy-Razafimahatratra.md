Group association schemes are commutative association schemes arising from the conjugacy classes of finite groups.
In this talk,  I will present results on Terwilliger algebras of group association schemes, with a particular focus on the notion of triple transitivity. I will first talk about the Terwilliger algebra of the symmetric group $$\operatorname{Sym}(n)$$, for small values of $$n$$. Then, I will show that the group association scheme of the Möbius group over finite field $$\mathbb{F}_q$$ is triply transitive if and only if $$q = 2$$.

This is partially based on joint work with Allen Herman and Roghayeh Maleki.
