name: Classifying Fourier Matrices of Small Rank via C-Algebras

Fourier matrices play a central role in modular data, fusion rings, and rational conformal field theory. In this talk, we classify complex Fourier matrices of ranks $$2$$ through $$5$$ under certain assumptions using techniques from $$C$$-algebra theory. We also develop criteria for identifying $$C$$-algebras that cannot arise from Fourier matrices.
