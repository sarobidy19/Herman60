### Minimum Number of Distinct Eigenvalues: Cycles, Distance-Regularity, and Hamming Graphs

#### Speaker: Shaun Fallat

We study the minimum number of distinct eigenvalues of a real symmetric matrix whose off-diagonal zero-nonzero pattern is described by a graph. Using idempotents arising from association schemes, we construct families of graphs that admit matrices with only two distinct eigenvalues, including the complements of certain hypercubes. As an application, we determine or derive bounds for the minimum number of distinct eigenvalues of several small distance-regular graphs, the line graphs of hypercubes, and Hamming graphs. This research is joint work with Himanshu Gupta, Allen Herman, and Johnna Parenteau.


### Witt’s Transitivity Theorem in vector spaces with non-definite inner product
#### Speaker: Douglas Farenick


If $$V$$ is an $$n$$-dimensional real, complex, or quaternionic inner product space of signature $$(p,q)$$, then, in joint work with student Chris Ramsey from 20 years ago, the isometry group $$U_{p,q}(V)$$ of
linear transformations $$T$$ on $$V$$ for which $$(Tv,Tw)=(v,w)$$, for all vectors $$v$$ and $$w$$, is shown to act transitively on the manifold $$S_{p,q}(V)=\{v\in V\,:\, (v,v)=1\}$$. A complete description of $$(p,q)$$ isometries allows one to determine the first two homotopy groups of $$S_{p,q}(V)$$ in the complex and quaterioninc cases, and of the connected components of $$S_{p,q}(V)$$ in the real case.
